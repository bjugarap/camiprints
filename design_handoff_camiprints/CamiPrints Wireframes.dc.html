Fixed on every step: header, title, stepper, “Step n of 6” sentence, and the Back / Continue bar at the same y-offset. Only the middle region swaps.
A11y: the stepper is an ordered list with aria-current="step"; on each advance focus moves to the step heading and the change is announced as “Step 3 of 6, Choose style”. Future steps are not clickable; completed steps are. Browser Back moves one step, never out of the flow.
